Privacy policy

my-social-blade is a private tool used by one person, its owner, to manage their own YouTube channels. Nobody else can sign in to it.

When the owner connects a channel, Google gives the tool access to that channel's videos, comments and analytics. The tool uses that access only to show the owner their own channels and to make the changes the owner asks for. The sign-in tokens and everything read from YouTube are kept on the owner's own computer at home. Nothing is sold, shared, sent to anyone else or used for advertising.

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time from their Google account's security page (Third-party apps with account access), or with Disconnect in the tool.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
